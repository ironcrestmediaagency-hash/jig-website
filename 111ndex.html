Straight talk

Who you're dealing with

We're investors, not real estate agents. We buy directly and don't represent you in the sale. Nothing gets listed on the MLS.

We may assign our contract. On some homes a partner cash buyer closes the purchase. You get the same price and closing date we agreed to, and we tell you this in writing before you sign.

Our offer is below full retail. We price in repairs, holding costs and our risk. In exchange you skip commissions, repairs, showings and waiting.

Take your time. You're welcome to have an attorney or advisor review our agreement before you sign.

Questions

Common questions

How fast can you close?

In 30 days or less from signing, and often sooner. If you need more time to move, we can set a later date.

Do I have to clean out or fix anything?

No. Sell it as-is. Take what you want and leave the rest.

What if I'm behind on my mortgage?

Call as soon as you can. The loan is paid off from the sale at closing, and the earlier we start, the more options you have before a foreclosure sale.

Can you buy an inherited house?

Yes. Whoever has legal authority to sell signs the agreement, usually the executor or the heirs. The title company confirms who that is.

Are there fees?

No agent commissions and no fees to us. Normal seller items like your loan payoff or back taxes come out of your proceeds at the title company, the same as any sale.

Buyer FAQ

Before you buy

What do you need from me to buy a deal?

Proof of funds or a lender letter, a signed assignment agreement, and an earnest money deposit with the title company.

How is the assignment fee paid?

It's shown on the closing statement and paid at closing through the title company. You see it before you sign.

Do you double close?

Most deals close by assignment. We can discuss a double close on a specific deal when it makes sense.

Can I use hard money or a loan?

Yes, as long as you can close on the contract timeline. Cash and hard-money buyers get priority on fast deals.
